## 3. Entry: who may claim the block

A survivor may claim fresh-0828 **only if** all of these hold.

1. **Screen survivor.** It is not killed by the screen rules its DECLARE pins. Those rules are at least as strict as `hunt23/RULES.md` Amendment 1 (all four sub-cells; explore-0814 alone and the September span pooled; the in-sample gate on all four sub-cells; ex-top-3 and ex-best-day), as `tools/arena_screen.py` applies them (the sha256 of `hunt23/RULES.md` is in the cohort notebook entry, and the pinned kit sha anchors the tool's behaviour).
2. **Lineage "none"** (section 1), one claimant per family.
3. **Family-adjusted screen p < 0.025 / m** [pinned], DEC-014 rule 1's Holm: "a family's Holm adjustment covers every cell it has scored across all its tries". The **entry statistic is the Holm-adjusted p of the survivor's cell among all n cells its family scored, across all tries, killed cells included** (a cell with no trips or no price counts in n with p = 1). Holm step-down: sort the family's cell p-values p_(1) <= ... <= p_(n); adjusted p_(i) = min(1, max over j <= i of (n - j + 1) x p_(j)). *[Forward pointer: Amendment 1, the last section of this file, says which cells enter this Holm at p = 1.]* The p tool is run on **every** cell the family scored. The cell p is defined as follows (quant-proof, PR #756 comment 6276924696, item 4).
   - **Stake and cell.** 1.9 s, **0.25 SOL (the kit's fixed stake; the p tool refuses any other stake)**, rent 0.
   - **Data.** For each of the four sub-cells (flat and pressure, each under strand rules "sc" and "total"), on the END leg (the engine's END-bound fill; worst-in-slot is report-only), pooled over the four canonical blocks (explore-0814 and the three September blocks) after exclusions. x_s is the per-attempt SOL vector that `arena_screen`'s `stat()` passes to `F.boot_lo` (`F` is `tools/arena_stack`), guarded reverts included. xbar is its mean.
   - **Draws.** B_E = 10,000 draws (below), seed 1, with a fresh `numpy.random.default_rng(1)` per sub-cell. Each draw is `m_b = x_s[rng.integers(0, n, n)].mean()`, the loop of `arena_stack.boot_lo` itself (one `rng.integers` call per draw). A test in the p tool's PR checks that the 5th percentile of the first 1,000 draws equals `F.boot_lo(x_s)`.
   - **Null.** H0 is mean at most 0, one-sided. `p_pct = (1 + #{m_b <= 0}) / (1 + B_E)`. `p_null = (1 + #{m_b - xbar >= xbar}) / (1 + B_E)`. The **sub-cell p is max(p_pct, p_null)**. The **cell p is the maximum over the four sub-cells**.
   - **Date-cluster p.** The kit's date-cluster resampler (`boot_lo(dsum, dn)`: resample UTC dates, statistic the pooled mean SOL per trade), B = 10,000, seed 1, with the same two tails and max rules. It is reported beside the trade-level p and decides only at the Holm step of section 6, not at entry.
   - **B_E = 10,000, one value for the whole cohort [pinned].** It is the Part 1 value, copied into the cohort notebook entry and never chosen, raised or lowered after the cohort's first price; Part 2 does not touch it. It matches DEC-014 rule 1 (10,000 seed-1 draws) and the #756 ruling. **Limits, plainly.** The smallest p the estimator can return is 1/(B_E + 1) = 1/10,001, so a family's best cell has an adjusted p of at least n/10,001, and it clears the bar only if n < 0.025 x 10,001 / m. **A family can clear the entry bar with at most 11 scored cells at m = 21, and at most 10 at m = 24.** A family with more scored cells than the limit at the frozen m cannot clear the bar. **A future cohort aiming at the bank should declare at most 10 cells per family** (every cell of every try, and of the same lineage). At m = 21 the cell-p bar is 0.0011905: a one-cell family clears with a tail count of 10 or fewer in 10,000 (p = 11/10,001 = 0.0011), and the Monte Carlo standard error near the bar is about 0.00034. A figure near the bar is not sharp.
   - **Tool.** The p tool lives in `tools/arena_screen.py` (a separate PR), report-only. It is a pure function of the kit's deciding-cell attempt rows at the kit sha the survivor was screened with, and the claim record pins that sha ("9041cfb or later" in the RULES is not enough; one exact sha). It must be merged with quant-proof's OK and pinned **before the cohort's first price**. A survivor whose DECLARE pins no kit sha that includes it cannot claim.
   - *[Forward pointer: Amendment 1, the last section of this file, adds conditions on the p defined above (small cells, `None` p, the first-1,000-draws flags, B and the kit sha equivalence check).]*
4. **At most 3 claimants, at most 1 per family** [pinned, mechanical]. The ranking statistic is the **same family-adjusted p as entry**. Within a family, the survivor with the smallest adjusted p; **a tie on the adjusted p (Holm's running maximum makes ties likely) goes to the cell earlier in its DECLARE's cell order** as notebooked. ## 5. Pre-read checks (before the lock; outcome-blind)

All run after the claim record and before any outcome row is opened. **A refusal before the lock spends nothing:** the block stays sealed and unread. One outcome-blind re-walk of a bad hour is allowed (EXP-015 section 6), recorded in a merged Part 2 amendment before the lock, then checks 1 to 5 run again. **Any failure after the lock spends the block** (section 6).

1. **Re-hash.** All **144/144** hours re-hashed against `VIEW.sha256` in each walker, with **0 flagged and 0 duplicate rows** (`tools/backfill_verify.py`, metadata and `--content --min-slots-per-hour 8000`).
2. **V map pin.** The V map is the Part 2 path@sha256, asserted at run time; the tool refuses if the file's sha256 differs. No fallback file unless a Part 2 amendment pins a named sha before the lock.
3. **V coverage > 99%** of the would-be prints, structure-only: of the PumpSwap WSOL-quote prints the claimed rules' entry predicates and pricing windows would read (decision slot to the end of the exit grace), the share whose pool has a V in the pinned map. 4. **Outcome-blind precount.** Per claimant, would-be attempts by UTC date from decision timestamps only (a tool that loads no fill, exit or P&L, as DEC-031 section 2). **Refuse that claimant below 100 attempts or on fewer than 5 UTC dates.** The precount also reports the block-edge exclusions (attempts whose exit or grace window would run past 2026-09-03T12) from decision timestamps and the rule's maximum hold, as an outcome-blind upper bound, and **refuses a claimant whose attempts net of that bound fall below 100**. ## 6. The read

**One locked job, one read, all claimants, once.** Nothing opens fresh-0828 before the lock. No retune and no peek: an interim read of a claimed rule on this block is a taint (DEC-014 (c); DEC-031 Amendment 3 item 4 (e)).

- **Deciding cell** (DEC-031 Amendment 2 and section 2): 1.9 s latency at 0.25 SOL (the kit's fixed stake), feed delay 7 slots, the E1 floor, 505,000 lamports per send, rent 0 on `filled` trips, flat 15% and pressure at slope scale 1, strand rules "sc" and "total", the engine's gap rules on. The frozen rule is run as frozen, including its 360-second pool-age filter. The 1.3 s leg and other stakes are reported and never decide.
- **Gate, on all four sub-cells** (flat and pressure, each under "sc" and "total"): at least 100 counted attempts (every entry decision, guarded reverts included); at least 5 distinct UTC dates with a majority of them positive; the CI90 lower bound of mean SOL per trade > 0 (1,000 draws, seed 1, 5th percentile, the gate's trade-level bound as `arena_score.cell_stats` calls it); total still > 0 after removing the top 3 trades.
- **Also, ex-best-day total > 0** on all four sub-cells.
- **Holm** [pinned]: alpha 0.05 over the **k claimants in the locked job** (k at most 3; k and the claimant list are in the `started` line). A claimant that fails the gate still counts in k, and **a claimant that is NOT_DECIDABLE on its own counts in k with p = 1**. For each, p_i is the cell p of section 3 item 3 (the maximum over the four sub-cells of max(p_pct, p_null), a fresh `default_rng(1)` per sub-cell) with **B = 10,000**, computed by **two resamplers**, trade-level and UTC-date cluster, both as defined there. Here x_s is the claimant's fresh-0828 attempts on the END leg after exclusions, at 1.9 s, 0.25 SOL, rent 0, in place of the four canonical blocks. Holm step-down runs on each resampler's p-values separately. **A claimant passes Holm only if it is rejected under both.** At k = 1 that is p at most 0.05; at k = 3 the smallest p must be at most 0.0167.
- **Disclosed: the date-cluster resampler has only 7 dates, two of them half days.** It is coarse, and a gate that needs it to reject at 0.0167 may be unreachable for a thin edge. The bar is not relaxed for that.
- **Reported beside the verdict, never deciding:** the rent-charged leg (Amendment 2 item 7); the worst-in-slot leg with its **no-guard-rescue companion**, with R7's canary figure taken as the lower of the two (`tools/arena_report_legs.py`); the UTC-date-cluster CI90 lower bound; per-date and per-walker tables; the 1.3 s leg; the multiplicity block of Amendment 3 item 3 (books dropped at the bank are disclosed and not counted in N; a passing book's bank read adds nothing to c, and its first arena read adds 1 at that read, item 7). The Amendment 2 item 6 mint fetch is not applied here (it reads current chain state): it is disclosed, not a deciding figure, and a canary still needs it on the arena book's mints (Amendment 3 item 6).
- **NOT_DECIDABLE** (after the lock), per claimant unless stated: any integrity failure, a missing or changed hour, a pin mismatch found late, an unresolved orphan or failed settlement or a counted trade without its pressure-leg P&L (DEC-014 Amendments 1 and 2), or a crash (these last apply to every claimant in the job). **Block-edge exclusions above the cap:** a claimant is NOT_DECIDABLE if, **in any one sub-cell**, `ex / (n + ex)` exceeds `arena_score.MAX_EXCLUDED_SHARE` = 0.05, where ex is the number of attempts excluded because their exit or grace window runs past the block's end (the kit's formula, `arena_screen.py` line 1040; the constant is imported at its line 84). The exclusion after the lock depends on exit paths, hence on outcomes, and the cap bounds that.
