## 3. Recipe and threshold

**Recipe.** [EXP-012 §3.1](EXP-012-migrate-entry-model-refreeze-prereg.md), re-fit: the 18 frozen features, LightGBM S2 `lgb_medium` (`num_leaves=15, min_data_in_leaf=20, learning_rate=0.05, rounds=100, feature_fraction=0.9, bagging_fraction=0.9, bagging_freq=1`, `scale_pos_weight=neg/pos`, `deterministic=True`, `num_threads=1`, `force_row_wise=True`), seed 1, `migrate` trigger, `tp50_sl30` exit. Table built with `buffer_hours=24`, `max_home_hours=12`. `tools.exp011_freeze._fit/_predict/_percentile` are imported unchanged. Frozen EXP-012 (model md5 `a1810d219ed61db64a396f40dc302ce5`, threshold 0.8030766588450794) is never touched.

**One fixed row universe for C1-C3.** Every migration with a k = 6, `tp50_sl30` row at the deciding costs of §4, **filled and MISS**, on the pool dates below (migrations before 2026-08-15T12 excluded). The file of (mint, UTC migration date, features) is built once, its sha256 is recorded in `data/tries.jsonl` and in Part 2 **before any fit**, and all three configurations read exactly it. Each config adds only its own label column.

**The three configurations (the entire try budget, §7).**

| Config | Label | Hyperparameters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C1** | EXP-012 §3.1 exactly: `1{pressure net > 0}` at the EXP-012 operating point (slot+1 start, 0.5 SOL, 0.0005 SOL per side, no V, pressure scale 1). The literal re-fit; isolates "more data". A mint in the universe whose EXP-012-pricing label cannot be computed is labelled 0, and the count is reported. | EXP-012's (`min_data_in_leaf=20`) |
| **C2** | The sign of expected net under `p_fail` at the deciding costs: label = 1 iff E = (1 − p_fail)·net' − p_fail·(505,000 lamports) > 0, where net' is the haircut net of both fees (§4) and p_fail comes from the pressure model at scale 1. **A MISS (no fill) is labelled 0.** | EXP-012's (`min_data_in_leaf=20`) |
| **C3** | **C2's label**, unchanged. | `min_data_in_leaf = 75`, fixed regardless of how many pool days exist, all else as EXP-012 |

Nothing about a config is chosen after seeing another's result.

**Folds and dates.** Folds, the gate bars, bootstrap clusters and the concentration bar all use **UTC dates of migration time**. The dates, with edge dates partial: P1 `2026-09-19 … 2026-09-27` (9); P2 `2026-08-15 … 2026-08-28` (14; migrations before 2026-08-15T12 and from 2026-08-28T12 excluded); P3+P4 `2026-09-03 … 2026-09-15` (13; 09-03 from 12:00Z, 09-09 shared by P3 before 12:00Z and P4 from 12:00Z as one fold, 09-15 to 12:00Z). **36 UTC dates, of which 27 are non-P1** (P2 plus P3+P4). "34 days" is hours divided by 24; the 36 dates include partial edge dates. Without P4 the dates are 9 + 14 + 7 = 30 (21 non-P1; P3 runs 09-03 to 09-09, so 09-09 then ends at 12:00Z) and the screen runs on that smaller pool, saying so in its first line.

**Threshold rule (pre-declared).** The entry fraction is fixed at the top 10%: threshold = the 90th percentile of the **pooled nested leave-one-day-out out-of-fold scores**, non-interpolating `index = round(0.90 * (n-1))`, as EXP-012 §3.1. Nested means: for each outer held-out UTC date d, the model and the threshold are built only from the other dates: one fit per inner fold (each other date held out in turn) to get the inner OOF scores whose p90 is that outer date's threshold, then one fit on all other dates to score d. The threshold of the final model is the p90 of the full pooled OOF scores over all training dates. 0.90 is **not** tuned. p80 and p95 are reported and never selected on.

**Purge, outer and inner.** Rows whose migration time falls within 35 minutes (the 30-minute exit cap plus slack) before the start of a held-out date are dropped from that fold's training; the same for the 35 minutes after its end. The **same purge applies in every inner fold**, around the inner held-out date.

**A pooled retrain cannot be scored on the days it trained on.** The final model, fit on every pool day, has no honest in-sample number; any per-day figure computed from it on those days is in-sample and is **never reported as performance**. The only evidence the pool can give is the nested out-of-fold scoring above (each day scored by a model that excluded it and its purge neighbours), and the one read in §6. Two further limits on the nested number: it trains on days both before and after each test day (no time order), so it cannot see drift, and the three configs plus all earlier tries on P1 make the best OOF number optimistic by selection, not just by noise. The honest read of the screen is "worth a confirmation read", never "has an edge".

## 4. Costs in every deciding cell

Every cell that can pass or fail a bar (nested screen §5, confirmation §6) uses all of these at once. Raw simulator rows are report-only.

- **Pricing:** PumpSwap virtual reserve V (about 17.58 SOL; `pumpswap-virtual-reserve` finding; a paper price that ignored V is not allowed). Pool fields come from a pool-field-only pre-pass.
- **Entry:** k = 6 slots **from the first PumpSwap print, not from migration**. k = 4 and k = 8 are reported (report-only).
- **Size:** 0.05 SOL. **Fee:** 505,000 lamports per side (500,000 priority + 5,000 base, DEC-019 Am.1). A MISS pays the fee.
- **Exit:** `tp50_sl30`, 30-minute cap, **exit lag 2** slots (`exit_land_k`). The exit re-check kept the frozen exit; the exit is not a tuning axis here. Lag 2 is still optimistic against the measured live exit leak.
- **Measured live haircut**, applied to each filled trade as in the backcheck: P = max(net0 + size, 0); entry gap g = 26.08 bps (mean sim-vs-live entry gap, job #175, n = 20 trades, median +1.03 bps); sell shortfall s = 16 bps of proceeds (the worst of the −11..−16 bps measured); net0' = net0 − P·(1 − (1 − g)(1 − s)) = net0 − P·0.0042038. Misses and censored rows are unchanged. MEV is not added; AMM concavity is ignored.
- **Fail models:** flat 15% and the pressure model at slope scale 1, both gating.
- **Gate computation:** `tools.paper_attention_promote.book_stats`, 1,000 bootstrap draws, seed 1, 5th percentile, cluster bootstrap by day as the gate does.

Not in the costs: entry noise (±300 bps), MEV, and the exit leak beyond lag 2. All three push the true number down. Preconditions: V maps for P3, P4 and fresh-0808 (the backcheck built `/data/mal/pumpswap-virtual/pool_v_0814.json` for P2 only; P1's was built for job #180). Each is a pool-field read with sha256 recorded; a missing V on more than 1% of migrating mints, or any primary-cell trade on a no-V pool, refuses the run, as in the backcheck.

## 5. Screen bars (exploration, stated before any computation)

Computed on the nested out-of-fold scores of each of C1, C2, C3, with §4's costs, under **both** fail models. A configuration passes only if **all** of bars 1-6 hold. **The screen counts a UTC date with no trade as not positive.**

1. **Gate shape on all 36 dates:** n entered ≥ 100; ≥ 5 UTC dates with a majority positive; CI90 lower bound of the mean > 0; total ex-top-3 > 0.
2. **The same four bars on the 27 non-P1 dates alone (P2+P3+P4).** These are the dates the frozen model and the earlier 74 tries never fit. P1's days were hammered, so they cannot carry the pass.
3. **Beats frozen EXP-012, paired on the same mints, on those 27 dates.** For each migration m, x_m = (s_new,m − s_frozen,m) · net_m, where s is the 0/1 selection (new: nested-OOF score ≥ its fold threshold; frozen: score ≥ 0.8030766588450794) and net_m is the haircut per-trade net under the leg. The mean of x_m over all migrations must be > 0 and its CI90 lower bound (date-cluster bootstrap, 1,000 draws, seed 1) must be > 0, under both fail models. **Also reported (not gating): the per-trade difference** (mean net per entered trade, new minus frozen) **and the Jaccard** of the two selected sets. The frozen model never trained on these dates, so the comparison is fair; the new model's folds trained on the other dates, which include P1. It is strict on purpose: if a 4x pool cannot beat a 9-day fit by a margin the CI can see, the pool bought nothing.
4. **Concentration, under both fail models, on all 36 dates and on the 27 non-P1 dates:** no UTC date contributes more than 20% of the positive-date total SOL, **and** total SOL excluding the best date is > 0. (EXP-012's holdout had 50.7% of flat SOL from its two oldest days.)
5. **Bar 2's mean > 0 under both legs on P2+P4 alone** (the August dates and the EXP-011 block dates). It restores the transfer screen DEC-017 asked for.
6. **September → August transfer.** Train on the September dates only (P1+P3+P4, 22 dates; the same recipe, the config's label and hyperparameters, threshold = p90 of the inner purged LODO OOF over those dates), score P2 (14 dates). The mean > 0 and a majority of P2's dates positive (a no-trade date is not positive), under both legs.

**Report-only, never gating and never selected on:**
- **Fast-only (pool A).** Its own LODO and p90, as DEC-017 (a) defined it. Required line in the report: "**pool A cannot change; DEC-017 (a) fast-only was +0.0005 [−0.0137, +0.0137]**". A pass here is not expected and a fail is not a new finding.
- The k = 4 and k = 8 versions; the raw (unhaircut) simulator result; the lag-0 result; the p80 and p95 thresholds; per-source and per-block tables; first-half vs second-half signs; the selected fraction.
- If the k = 6 cell passes and both k = 4 and k = 8 are negative, the PR that reports it says "knife-edge" in its first line.

## 7. Multiplicity, tries and the winner's curse (read before trusting a PASS)

- **Tries cap: at most 3 configurations (C1-C3), each logged.** A `started` line goes to `data/tries.jsonl` (key `exp015_c1..c3`) before any fit, carrying the row-universe sha256; each config's result line follows. The run refuses if any prior `exp015_*` line exists. Each config counts as a try on **every** pool it touches: the 9-day pool goes from 74 to 77, `explore-0814` from 1 (DEC-017 (a)) + 6 (backcheck) = 7 to 10, and P3 and P4 start at 3 each (P3 additionally carries EXP-012's one confirmation read).
- **Winner's curse on the screen.** The recipe was tuned on P1 (best of 6 cells for EXP-012, then 68-74 tries of further ideas, all flat). The configs are chosen on the same dates they are OOF-scored on. The screen is a filter for "worth one confirmation read" and its numbers are upward-biased. A pass is not evidence for an edge.
- **Multiplicity across EXP-012, EXP-013, EXP-014 and EXP-015.** Each family is read on its own block with k = 1, so DEC-014's Holm step-down (k books or cells read together at one review) does not formally pool them. They are still one lab hunting in one data set. At the gate's nominal 5% one-sided false-positive rate, and if no real edge exists anywhere, the chance that at least one of **four** reads (EXP-012's holdout, EXP-013, EXP-014, EXP-015) passes is 1 − 0.95⁴ ≈ **18.5%**; adding EXP-012's forward read makes **five** reads, 1 − 0.95⁵ ≈ **22.6%**. (EXP-014 now waits for another block, but its screen and read are still part of the lab's search.) EXP-012's holdout PASS is one of those draws.
- **No document may cite the EXP-012 and EXP-015 PASSes as two confirmations.** EXP-015 trains on EXP-012's holdout block and on the data EXP-012 was tuned on; the two are not independent.
- **Holm in the confirmation.** The 0.0167 bar in §6 (e) applies Holm's first threshold for k = 3; k = 3 counts the three unread families EXP-013, EXP-014, EXP-015, and EXP-012's holdout already spent its α. The resampler is the `book_stats` resampler and the date-cluster resampler, 10,000 draws, seed 1; both must pass. If any two of EXP-012's forward book, EXP-013, EXP-014 and EXP-015 are put forward in the same promote decision, DEC-014 (b) applies with k = the number put forward (10,000 draws, seed 1, both fail models).
- **The paired bar's reference is already seen.** The frozen model's per-day results on `explore-0814` come from the backcheck (job #207) and on fresh-0903 from the EXP-012 read. They will be known when the screen runs. The plan does not change in response, and this is disclosed.
- **Seeds:** seed 1 everywhere. No seed search.
