## 2. Scope
1. **Rotation books.** A rotation book is a frozen rule with a live executor path, run under this DEC with its own `bot_id` (never `floor-lane-1`, `h5-lane`, `l1-floor-v2` or an `hv4-` id). DEC-035 §3 holds: a code or prompt change is a new `bot_id`.
2. **Separate wallet and key.** All rotation books trade from one rotation wallet, separate from FLOOR's. Helm creates it and places its key root-only at the path the rotation unit binds. No Claude session reads, prints or copies it. The owner funds it.
3. **Separate ledger and limits.** Rotation books share one rotation ledger and lock, at paths that are not FLOOR's. They are limited by the §5 constants, the wallet-wide STOP and A3 pauses (item 5). No rotation book can take FLOOR's wallet room, count in FLOOR's ledger or trip FLOOR's budget, daily stop, open cap or STOP file. The one link is read-only: the same-pool refusal (§9 item 6) reads which pools floor-lane-1 holds open.
4. **FLOOR unchanged.** floor-lane-1's config, ledger, wallet, limits, decision host, executor tree, decay monitor (QP-RULING-1118-floor-brake §A) and every DEC-035 amendment for it are unchanged by this DEC.
5. **Stops.** Rotation executors honour the wallet-wide STOP Helm uses (`/var/lib/mal-live/STOP`) and A3 pauses (DEC-035 §4), as FLOOR does. The rotation lane STOP file and per-book STOP files stop rotation books only. Any session may create them (DEC-035 §2 item 6 pattern). Removal, as in DEC-035 §2 item 6: a STOP written by the lifetime budget stop (§5 item 7) survives restarts and only a recorded owner budget change removes it; every other rotation STOP only the manager removes, after a notebook note. The month budget (§5 item 6) writes no STOP file: it is a pre-send refusal that the code lifts at the next month's 00:00Z.
6. **Hosts.** Executors run on mal-fast-0 only. DEC-035 §7 (seal overlap declaration) applies to each rotation book before its first live send.

## 3. One method card per book
Each book has a method card, `docs/rotation/books/<bot_id>.md`, copied from [docs/rotation/METHOD-CARD-TEMPLATE.md](../docs/rotation/METHOD-CARD-TEMPLATE.md). The card is a checklist of questions, not one bar. It answers, for that book:
1. **Mechanism:** who loses money to it, what would stop it, how we would see that.
2. **Which sim:** open simulation book (DEC-035 Am.9 §1), replay on held-out hours, or a FLOOR twin or sibling; and why that sim fits.
3. **What makes the sim believable:** fill, latency and cost assumptions matched to FLOOR's live reconciliation (and, while FLOOR's control book is SIM OFF, the haircut used for the measured live-minus-sim bias); V-priced PumpSwap math; a per-token fee check against landed fills; both fail legs; the trial count logged and the estimate shrunk for it; ex-top-3 and per-day totals; net basis at today's cost table (§8).
4. **Believe-the-sim criteria** for that book, set from its mechanism (a rare-event book needs more days, a frequent one more trades).
5. **"Keep running longer"** if the sim is negative but still plausible: what makes it plausible, how much longer (fixed in advance, one extension unless a new card version says why), what ends it early. Parameters stay fixed; a change is a new book.
6. **Kill and retire rule:** its loss cap inside §5, its decay calibration (§6), anything mechanism-specific that stops it at once.
7. **Decision host path:** DSL-expressible (FORGE DSL spec on the rotation executor, no new code) or own code (host and port as a T1 code PR with an md5 decision-equivalence replay proof).
8. **Ladder plan** and its up-step conditions (§5 item 1).

DEC-035 Am.9 §2 honesty rules apply to every sim cited on a card.

## 4. Go-live condition
A rotation book goes live only when all of these hold:
1. Its method card is complete, and its sim has run as the card says.
2. Its shrunk net sim mean per trade (card §3 item 6) is **> 0 under both fail legs**, and **μ0 > 0 at the first rung** (μ0 exactly as §6 item 3 defines it: pressure leg, winsorized, net at that stake, at the card's deciding latency cell). A book that fails either keeps its sim running (card §5, "keep running longer"); it does not go live. This is how the owner's "leave it running for a bit longer" applies. So μ0 > 0 holds by construction for every live book at the rung it trades at (§5 item 1 repeats the μ0 test at each up-step target).
3. The manager has written a **belief record** in the book's file: the card version, the sim results (labelled in-sample or forward, both fail legs, ex-top-3, per day, "best of n; treat as shrunk"), why the believe-the-sim criteria are met, the first rung, and the instance config sha256.
4. Quant-proof OK and REVIEWER APPROVE on that record at the exact head of the PR that adds it (T1, §7).
5. The §5 envelope has room (wallet funded, books live < cap, month and lifetime room).

**Not from replay alone, except** when the card argues why a forward sim adds nothing for this mechanism, the replay hours are held out (checked against `docs/HOLDOUT_LEDGER.md`, never in-sample hours), and QP accepts both. Live rotation trades are never pooled with sim trades. Anything beyond the envelope (above 0.25, a gate claim, a larger budget) still needs the CLAUDE.md promotion gate on a DEC-035 §6 registration, or an amendment with the owner's word.

## 5. Envelope (all numbers are PROPOSALS [owner to set])
Every full-wipe bound below counts each position (open and new) at its stake plus the per-position fee reserve **R = 3,860,000 lamports** (DEC-035: rent 2,100,000 + send 160,000 + sell reserve 1,600,000), in DEC-035 §2 item 1 / Am.3 form: a new entry is sent only if realized − Σ over open positions (stake + R) − (new stake + R) > −limit. The escape paths of DEC-035 Am.6 apply to every bound in this section (per-book, daily, month, lifetime): rows that never reach the ledger (backlogged rows block entries), estimated fill rows, and exits whose cost exceeds the 1,600,000 sell reserve.
1. **Stake ladder.** Rungs (lamports) 50,000,000, 100,000,000, 125,000,000 and 250,000,000; nothing below 0.05. Every new book starts at 0.10. Rungs 0.125 and 0.05 are reached only by the decay ladder (§6 item 5). **Up-steps** go one rung at a time, on this path only: 0.05 → 0.10, 0.10 → 0.25, 0.125 → 0.25. 0.125 → 0.10 is never a step. Each up-step needs, **at the current rung**: at least 20 closed live trades at that rung since the book's last stake change; realized SOL after all fees ≥ 0 over those trades; no decay warning or alarm active; live entry slip inside the card's sim assumption; μ0 > 0 at the target rung (§6 item 3, recomputed at that stake); and a T1 step record with QP OK (§7) [owner to set]. The executor enforces the one-rung path, the count and the record sha256 (§9 item 2); the step record enforces the rest. This is a step condition, not edge evidence: with no edge (true mean −c = −0.038 s at 0.10) and the brake ruling's assumed σ ≈ 0.28 s (QP-RULING-1118-floor-brake A3, an assumption), P(sum of 20 ≥ 0) ≈ 27%. Above 0.25: not in this envelope; it needs the owner's word and a price-impact estimate at that stake.
2. **Per-book loss cap (full-wipe).** 0.40 SOL lifetime for a book with no 0.25 start row in the rotation ledger; 0.75 SOL once the book has a 0.25 start row [owner to set]. Arithmetic for a fresh book (realized 0): at 0.10, 3 × 103,860,000 = 311,580,000 is admitted and 4 × 103,860,000 = 415,440,000 > 400,000,000 is refused, so 3 open; at 0.25 with the 0.75 cap, 2 × 253,860,000 = 507,720,000 is admitted and 3 × 253,860,000 = 761,580,000 > 750,000,000 is refused, so 2 open. When the book has no open position and the pre-send refuses an entry at its current rung on realized alone (book realized − (stake + R) ≤ −cap), the book's STOP file is written and the book retires (§6). While positions are open, refusals at the cap are counted and wait for those exits. A book re-lived under the same `bot_id` keeps its lifetime cap and its realized; a new `bot_id` gets a fresh cap, but the month and lifetime budgets still bind.
3. **Per-book open cap.** At most 4 open positions [owner to set]. Under item 2 it does not bind for a fresh book (3 open at 0.10, 2 at 0.25); it binds once realized gains widen the item 2 room.
4. **Lane-wide daily stop across rotation books.** 0.30 SOL [owner to set], measured as in `FloorLaneLedger.evaluate` (marked and cost-basis arms) over rotation books only, checked before an entry. One UTC day can lose more: the bound is about 0.30 SOL plus the open exposure admitted before the stop, ≤ 0.30 + 1.00 + 10 × 0.00386 ≈ 1.339 SOL, further capped by the month's full-wipe room (item 6) and, at first funding, by the wallet above its floor (1.5 − 0.25 = 1.25 SOL). Correlated dumps hit several books at once; this stop is the guard.
5. **Total open exposure.** Sum of open rotation stakes ≤ 1.00 SOL [owner to set] (10 at 0.10, or 4 at 0.25).
6. **Exploration budget (month, full-wipe).** Month realized (all rotation books, the current UTC calendar month) − Σ open (stake + R) − (new stake + R) > −1.50 SOL [owner to set]. Refusals carry `rotation_month_budget`; the code lifts them at the next month's 00:00Z (no STOP file). Basis: under item 2 a failed book loses at most 0.40 SOL at 0.10 or 0.75 SOL after a 0.25 step, so 1.50 SOL is about 3.75 failed 0.10 books, or 2 failed 0.25 books, a month.
7. **Lifetime budget (full-wipe).** Rotation lifetime realized − Σ open (stake + R) − (new stake + R) > −3.00 SOL [owner to set], so lifetime loss cannot pass 3.00 SOL with the open book wiped out. When no rotation position is open and the pre-send would refuse an entry at the lowest rung on realized alone (lifetime realized − (50,000,000 + R) ≤ −3.00 SOL), the rotation lane STOP is written; only a recorded owner budget change removes it (§2 item 5).
8. **Wallet slice per book.** One book's open (stake + R) ≤ 40% of the rotation wallet's funded amount [owner to set]: at 1.5 SOL funded, 0.60 SOL, so at 0.25 at most 2 open (2 × 0.25386 = 0.50772; 3 × = 0.76158). Wallet floor 0.25 SOL held once [owner to set].
9. **Books live at once.** At most 3 [owner to set] (review load and the ≤5 active lanes board).
10. **Funding order.** FLOOR first. The rotation wallet is funded only from SOL the owner earmarks for it, after FLOOR's wallet covers its Am.12 capacity, unless the owner says otherwise. Proposed first funding 1.5 SOL [owner to set].

## 6. Decay and retirement
1. **Data.** Per closed live trade, r = realized SOL after all fees, nothing dropped (as `tools/floor_decay_monitor.py` derives it from the ledger), and x = r / s with s the stake of that trade. A stake change starts a new CUSUM population; the retire check uses all live trades of the book.
2. **Winsorize (CUSUM only).** w = clip(x, L, U), with L and U the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of x in the book's own sim [card may set others, with reason]. Reason: profit in these books comes from rare large wins (FLOOR: one missed take-profit, +0.5503, was 87% of a +0.6356 missed list); raw-SOL rules alarm on normal losing runs and trust lucky runs.
3. **CUSUM (one-sided, down).** μ0 = the book's shrunk sim mean of w on the **pressure** fail leg, net of costs at the book's **current stake** (impact recomputed for that stake, §8), at the deciding latency cell named on the card. §4 item 2 tests this same quantity at the first rung, and §5 item 1 at each up-step target, so μ0 > 0 at every rung a live book trades at. If μ0 at a decay rung is ≤ 0, the decay step to it is a book STOP instead. The card carries μ0, h_w and h_a for each rung the book can reach; the item 4 calibration is redone per rung. μ1 = min(0, μ0 − δ) with δ > 0 on the card, so μ1 < μ0 and κ = (μ0 − μ1) / 2 > 0; S_0 = 0, S_t = max(0, S_{t−1} + (μ0 − κ) − w_t).
4. **Calibration per book (in control = μ0).** Build 10,000 bootstrap paths (seed 1): resample whole UTC sim days with replacement and concatenate them until a path has at least 100 trades, then truncate to 100. Re-centre every path on μ0 (w − mean_sim(w) + μ0) before running the CUSUM, so the false-alarm rates hold for a book at μ0, not at the unshrunk sim mean. Set h_w and h_a so that P(S reaches h_w within 100 trades) = 20% and P(S reaches h_a within 100 trades) = 5% [owner to set; QP to confirm]. With fewer than 10 sim days that have trades, day blocks are not used: trades are resampled one by one, and the card says the rates then understate clustering. The calibration run's path and sha256 go on the card before go-live.
5. **Decay ladder** (manager, as records, §7). Only defined rungs; nothing below 0.05:

   | Stake now | Warning (S ≥ h_w) | Alarm (S ≥ h_a) |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | 0.25 | 0.125 | 0.05 |
   | 0.125 | 0.05 | 0.05 |
   | 0.10 | 0.05 | 0.05 |
   | 0.05 | book STOP, retire to paper | book STOP, retire to paper |

   Any STOP trigger the card names also stops the book. S resets at each stake change. There is no automatic step up: the way back is the §5 item 1 up path (0.05 → 0.10 → 0.25; 0.125 → 0.25), one rung at a time, each step with 20 closed trades at the current rung since the last stake change, realized ≥ 0 over them, and a T1 step record.
6. **Retire to paper (raw basis).** At every close from closed live trade 50 on (no end), the shrunk mean m = (n · mean(x) + n0 · μp) / (n + n0), with x raw (all trades, nothing dropped, not winsorized, so the prior and the data are on one scale); μp = −c̄, where c̄ is the mean over the book's trades of the round-trip cost c at each trade's stake (§8: about 0.036 at 0.05, 0.038 at 0.10, 0.040 at 0.125, 0.053 at 0.25, in stake units), the no-edge prior; n0 from the card (default 25 [owner to set]). Example: at n = 50, n0 = 25, c̄ = 0.038, m ≥ 0 needs mean(x) ≥ 0.019 s. If m < 0, or the per-book loss cap is hit, the book's instance stops and it goes back to an open sim. Heavy-tail note: one large win moves mean(x) a lot, so m can sit above 0 on luck and fall below it after one missed win; this check is a stop rule, not a test of edge. Re-live only with a new belief record naming what is new (§5 item 2 on the cap).
7. **Not adopted here.** Market-heat and bad-hour brakes (SOL stress, entry clustering, rolling realized, exposure cap) are not rules of this DEC. They go through the registered overlay study of QP-RULING-1118-floor-brake §B (deciding set E2 entries in [2026-11-18T12:00Z, 2026-11-26T00:00Z), read at or after 2026-11-27T02:00Z). A passing overlay may later be proposed for rotation books by its own record with QP OK.
8. **Power.** These rules are brakes, not tests. At 20 to 100 trades they detect a collapse, not the difference between a small edge and none, and they say nothing about whether a book has an edge.

## 8. Costs (sources named; estimates and model figures labelled)
- **Send fee:** 160,000 lamports per send (FLOOR live; DEC-035 capacity blocks). A round trip has at least two sends: 0.64% of stake at 0.05, 0.32% at 0.10, 0.256% at 0.125, 0.128% at 0.25.
- **Pool fee (estimate):** about 1.2% of stake per leg (probe-final-2026-10-07 line 164: the buy row's logged `pool_fee_est_lamports`, about 600,000 at 0.05 in October PumpSwap pools; the sell leg is assumed equal), about 2.4% round trip. Per-token fee check on each card (§3).
- **Rent:** 2,100,000 reserved; measured refundable at 1,513,840 lamports. Both executors close the token accounts, so rent is net 0 when reclaimed; unreclaimed rent is a cost and is added to c.
- **Price impact (model, V + q ≈ 18 SOL constant product, not fills):** round trip 2 s² / (18 + s) SOL. At 0.10: 0.001105 SOL, 1.105% of stake (DEC-035 Am.4 item 7, Am.6). At 0.25: 0.00685 SOL, 2.74% (DEC-035 Am.6 Erratum 2 item 4 formula; the review's "about 2.8%" rounds up). At 0.05: 0.554%; at 0.125: 1.379%. In SOL per trade it scales with the stake squared. Deeper pools have less: the probe's V-priced re-sim gives +22.9 bps of entry impact at 0.25 against 0.05 (probe line 176), so the 18 SOL model is the conservative case.
- **All-in round trip c = send fee + pool fee + impact** (rent excluded because it is refunded; arithmetic, not a measurement), before any failed-send cost: 0.05: 0.64 + 2.4 + 0.554 = 3.594%; 0.10: 0.32 + 2.4 + 1.105 = 3.825%; 0.125: 0.256 + 2.4 + 1.379 = 4.035%; 0.25: 0.128 + 2.4 + 2.740 = 5.268%. This is c in §6 item 6 unless the card measures a different number.
- **The probe's "2.59% at 0.5" is a fee-only projection** that scales the fixed fees; the probe note states price impact is not in it (probe lines 174, 176). It also carries the probe's 1,000,000-lamport priority per trip (2.00% at 0.05, probe line 63), not FLOOR's 160,000 per send. That it is not a measurement and not about deeper pools is the review's conclusion (rotation-plan-review.md line 17). The probe's own inference with sell-leg impact is about 3.62% at 0.5.
