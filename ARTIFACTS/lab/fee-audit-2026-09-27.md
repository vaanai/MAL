### Identity the scorer uses

Direct route, portal 0, size `S`, one sell. `v_buy` and `v_sell` are ppm from `pumpswap_sol_fee_ppm` at that leg's market cap.

```
net_in    = S * (1_000_000 - v_buy) // 1_000_000
sell_gross = tokens * quote // (base + tokens)     # constant-product output, before the sell fee
sol_out   = sell_gross * (1_000_000 - v_sell) // 1_000_000
net0      = sol_out - S                            # priority not included
gross     = sell_gross - net_in                    # = net0 + buy_fee + sell_fee
buy_fee   = S - net_in
sell_fee  = sell_gross - sol_out
```

So `net0 = gross - buy_fee - sell_fee`. A flat price makes `gross ≈ 0`: gross is the price edge before both venue fees, measured against SOL that entered the pool (`net_in`), not against wallet size. It does not already subtract the sell fee.

Flat 15% (`mixed_net`), priority `P` lamports per side:

```
raw  = net0 - P * pri_sides
flat = raw                         if the attempt missed (pri_sides = 1)
flat = 0.85 * raw + 0.15 * (-P)    if it sent
```

Means divide by n = sends + misses. Censored exits are omitted (3 of 2,950 migrations). Misses add 0 to gross. The same gross is reported at every priority.

Realized-only means in this cell: gross **+4.665%**, buy+sell fees **2.511%** of size, `net0` **+2.154%**. Population gross is `1189/2947 * 4.665% = 1.882%`.

### Why 1.88% and −1.95% are 3.8 points apart

The 6.5% figure is a full direct round trip on every attempt: about 2.48% venue if both legs are 1.25% and price is unchanged, plus `2 * 0.001 / 0.05 = 4%` priority. This cell does not look like that.

- 1,758 attempts never pay venue. They contribute 0 gross and `−P` net.
- Venue on a fill is `buy_fee + sell_fee`, and the sell fee is `v_sell * sell_gross`, not 1.25% of size. Across fills that averages 2.511% of size.
- Priority is two sides on a send and one side on a miss. The 15% fail slice of a send is charged one priority, not two.

At priority 0.0001 the reported **+0.47%** is above `1.88% − 2.48%`. That subtraction takes a full venue round trip off a mean that is already 60% zeros. Fills average **+4.665%** gross. After 2.511% venue and `2 * 0.0001 / 0.05 = 0.40%` priority, the flat-15% mix on those fills, blended with misses at −0.20%, is +0.47%.

`FeeConfig` is bump, admin, flat fees, then a tier vec. Each tier is a u128 market-cap threshold plus lp / protocol / creator as u64 basis points. Canonical pools and bonding curves use the tiers. `Global.fee_basis_points` is the fallback only when `FeeConfig` is absent.

### Bonding curve

One tier, threshold 0: creator **30** + protocol **95** + LP **0** = **125 bps (1.25%)** at every market cap. A bonding buy and a bonding sell pay the same 1.25%. It does not step down as the curve fills. Flat fees on the same account are the same 125 bps. A second one-tier list on the account repeats it.

`Global.fee_basis_points` is **95** and `creator_fee_basis_points` is **5** (100 bps combined). That is the fallback, not the live charge. `FeeConfig` is set, and the program uses it (`computeFeesBps` in `@pump-fun/pump-sdk`). Pool migration fee on Global is 15,000,001 lamports (0.015 SOL), the graduation rent in the docs, not a trade fee.

Graduation virtual quote is 115,005,359,056 lamports, market cap **410.88 SOL** (`graduated_swing.py`). A buy there pays **1.25%**. A sell at +50%, market cap **616.32 SOL**, pays **1.20%**. The total does change with market cap: 1.25% down to 0.30% at 98,240 SOL, on the same steps as `PUMPSWAP_SOL_FEE_TIERS`.

The trader pays the sum. LP stays in the pool on a buy and leaves the pool on a sell (`pumpswap_pool_quote_delta`). Creator bps go to the creator, or to holders on a holder-rewards coin. That changes the recipient, not the amount (`HOLDER_REWARDS_README.md`). Cashback, which refunds the creator slice to the trader, is deprecated: `create_v2` rejects new cashback coins. `Global.buyback_basis_points` and the PumpSwap global-config copy are **5000**. That field is `update_buyback_config`, a split of collected fees, not an extra tier on top of `FeeConfig`.

Non-canonical pools use flat fees on the PumpSwap `FeeConfig`: creator 0, protocol 5, LP 25 = **0.30%**. Migrated pump.fun pools are canonical and use the tiers. The same account's second 25-tier list is the USDC schedule (first break 59,000 USDC). The latency scorer drops non-wSOL prints.
