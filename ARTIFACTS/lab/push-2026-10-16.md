## 3. What this means for the plan

1. The library cannot reach the goal by tuning, because size is the binding limit. Every line's reported exploration mean (none is an established edge) is half gone by about 0.1–1.7 SOL per trade.
2. Ways to get there:
   - (a) A new mechanism with roughly 10× the trade count at small size, or an edge that does not depend on pool depth. None was found today.
   - (b) Run several small, uncorrelated lines in parallel at stakes under their impact limit. The shortlist sums to about 0.2–0.3 SOL/day.
   - (c) More capital behind a gate-passing book, so that stake × lines can grow without growing per-trade size.
   - The realistic near-term route is (b) plus (c): get one or two lines through the gate quickly at small stakes, then widen.
