## Caveats

- **Re-read dates.** These are the same 27 non-P1 dates EXP-015 read; the frozen-book family count on these 27 dates is 7 families (EXP-015, EXP-016, EXP-017, EXP-018, EXP-019, EXP-020 report-only, EXP-014 v2; `EXP/EXP-014-mig15-pumpswap-selector-plan.md` Amendment 7), and EXP-017 is the 4th to read outcomes, after EXP-015, EXP-018 and EXP-019 (the screen's per-pool prior-try disclosure: P1 81, P2 18, P3 10, P4 10 logged tries). Holm covers only H3 and H4; the lab-wide alpha dilution is not corrected. Treat every p as optimistic.
- Winner's curse: the frozen EXP-012 cell is itself best-of-many; filters on top of it inherit that selection.
- H3's gate reads outcomes of earlier migrations only; its left-censor removes the first 24 h of each block (3,831 left-censored rows).
- The cached cells are 0.05 SOL; sized cells (H4, C0) come from the separate re-simulation (job #319). P1 dates are report-only and not scored here.
- `result_H3.json` and `result_H4.json` in the out-dir are `result.v1` records (the coordinator copies them to `~/data/results/`); `RUN.record.json` is run status only.
- **CI recipe.** Every CI quoted here is from `screen.json` (book-level, mint-clustered bootstrap). The per-trade CIs in `result_H3.json` / `result_H4.json` differ slightly (for example H4 flat CI lower bound -0.0013671376302256276 there vs -0.0013667118605363983 here) and are also below 0.
- **Holm order.** Holm assigns thresholds by p rank: H4 (lower p) gets 0.025 and H3 gets 0.05. The plan text implied the reverse; no ordering rejects either.
