### Q6. Replacing the P0 20/day drop with a non-killing rule  VERDICT: GO WITH CONDITIONS (gate unchanged)

Why the drop existed: the drop rule never protected the gate. It protected N and the calendar. A book that cannot reach 100 attempts on 5 dates before its window ends still costs 1 in N, spends a NOT_DECIDABLE, and buys no evidence. A low rate is not a statistical problem in itself. Every book is still read at 100 attempts with the same CI, ex-top-3 and 5-date tests.

Minimal honest replacement (a DEC-031 amendment that replaces Am.3 item 1's drop rule, outcome-blind, QP OK on the final head plus a reviewer verdict, applying only to books registered after the merge):
 S1. P0 stays as a structure-only count on at least 3 full closed UTC days, at the registration-candidate sha, before the clock. It decides only two things:
   - a floor: the book is not registered if P0 is below 2.0 would-be attempts a day on average, or if any P0 day has 0;
   - its HORIZON H (S2).
   The existing hard-gap and data_loss > 1 h rule of Am.3 item 1 stays: a P0 window that fails it is NOT_DECIDABLE and the book is not registered on it.
 S2. Horizon, pinned in the bots.json line before the clock and never extended: H_days = min(90, max(14, ceil(1.5 x 100 / P0_rate_per_day))), further capped by any end-seal or window end that applies to the book (for H5 books, the 2026-11-06T00Z arena end-seal or its successor). The read stays DEC-031 s2's: the first 00:00Z boundary with at least 100 counted attempts on at least 5 UTC dates, counted by the outcome-blind decision-timestamp tool. If that boundary has not come by clock + H, the book ENDS NOT_DECIDABLE at clock + H, final for the bot_id, with counts only. There is no early read on fewer than 100, no extension, and no re-registration of the same rule to "continue" it (a re-registration is a new bot_id, adds to N, and its earlier end is disclosed).
 S3. A cap, not a target: at 2 a day, 100 attempts take about 50 days, so H is 75 days. That is long. Regime drift and structure changes accrue over it, which is why S1 and S2 need Q3 (or the current A3 rule) in force. A slow book also spends more redeploys inside its window, and every record-only redeploy is disclosed at its read.
 S4. N and c: every registered book counts in N from its clock until forever, including books that end at H as NOT_DECIDABLE (Am.3 item 3). Books turned away at the S1 floor are disclosed and not counted, as P0 drops are now. A wider floor means more registered books and a larger N, so the plain line 1 - 0.95^N rises (40% at N = 10, 79% at N = 30). c changes only through screen passes (unchanged). Slow books are read late, so under the running c of Am.3 item 2 a late confirmation faces a smaller 0.05/c. Say so in each read. Parallel low-frequency books are welcome, but each one costs 1 in N, so count them before registering them.
 S5. Unchanged: the promotion gate (100 OOS trades, at least 5 UTC dates with a majority positive, CI90 lower bound > 0, ex-top-3 > 0, under flat 15% and pressure scale 1); s2's one outcome-blind read per bot_id; step 4's confirmation (100 attempts on 5 further dates at p <= 0.05/c); the 5% exclusion cap; the seals. A low-frequency book's confirmation also needs 100 further attempts, so a book at 2 a day needs about 100 days from clock to a confirmed screen pass. State that in its registration, so that nobody plans live money on a shorter path.
 S6. Books dropped at P0 under the old 20/day rule are not revived by this amendment. Each needs a new registration under S1 and S2, with the old drop disclosed.
