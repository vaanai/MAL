### Starting **October 13**
README.md: - **Pump coins paired with pump coins can be quote mints.** Starting **October 13**, `create_v2` can pair a new coin with a pump coin that is itself paired with a pump coin (SOL → A → B → C). You pass one extra group of remaining accounts for each coin below the quote coin, and the starting price is computed from the whole chain live. [Creating a coin paired with a pump coin](docs/instructions/CREATE_WITH_PUMP_COIN_QUOTE.md#depth)

### Depth 2 is enabled on October 13
docs/instructions/CREATE_WITH_PUMP_COIN_QUOTE.md: pump.fun sets how deep a new coin may go (`Global.max_curve_depth`). **Depth 2 is enabled on October 13**, so from then on Q can be a coin that is itself paired with a pump coin.
