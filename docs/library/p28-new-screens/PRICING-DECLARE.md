## Pricing model (verified in code: tools/arena_stack.py `mint_states`/`round_trip`, tools/arena_screen.py `legs`)
- Universe: WSOL PumpSwap rows with V in [17.5e9, 17.7e9] lamports (kit map `pool_v_0814`, then `pool_v_0909`); pool age >= 360 s enforced by the kit; both ideas additionally require age >= 7,200 s from the pool's first print in the block (as the counts did).
- Reserves are PRE-trade per print (`mint_states` docstring: "reserves PRE-trade"); the state used for a fill is `QX[je] = quote_reserve_pre + V`, `BX[je] = base_reserve_pre`, V added to the quote side. Decision spot = post-trade price of the last print at slot <= decision slot, `(q +/- sol*(0.99|1.01) + V) / (b -/+ tok)`.
- Entry: decision at the rule's slot `sd` (a print slot; the rule uses rows with slot <= sd only). Latency leg 1.9 s deciding: `k = ceil(1.9 / slot_seconds)` slots; the buy fills on the PRE-trade state of the first print of the pool at slot >= `sd + k + 1` (so at least 1.9 s after the decision). Tier fee `fee_ppm(Qe, Be)`; haircut in 0.2608%, out 0.16%; E1 floor; guard 1.15 (buy refused, pnl = -send, if net/tokens > 1.15 x decision spot). The feed delay of 7 slots applies to the exit clock.
- Exit: take profit x1.12, stop x0.92 (on the POST-trade price of prints at slot >= `sd + k + 1`), else time exit 900 s; sells land by the kit's exit lag from the exit decision slot. These are the FLUSH exits from hunt29 P4 (`docs/library/hunt29-p4/DECLARE.md`, I1), reused unchanged for CALL-SYNC (no new exit invented, no exit tuning).
- Costs: send 160,000 lamports per leg (DEC-031 Amendment 8; the same stack hunt29 P4/P9 used via a `screen_cfg` wrap) DECIDES. Sensitivity reported beside, never deciding: send 505,000 (the kit default). Rent 0 deciding (DEC-031 Am.2: the sell closes the ATA; a stranded trip pays 2,039,280); the rent-charged leg is reported beside. Strand rule `total` deciding (`sc` is reported).
- Fail models: flat 15% and pressure-fail at slope 1. The pressure intercept is refit on the BASE cell's trips; a subset of a base cell (kept or vetoed set) uses the base cell's intercept, not its own refit. Every mean-based kill rule is applied under both models.
- Stake 0.25 SOL deciding. Statistic: SOL per trade (the kit's `x`, lamports / 1e9). Bootstrap 1,000 draws, seed 1, 5th percentile of the mean: trade level `arena_stack.boot_lo` (reported); UTC-date clustered `arena_screen.boot_lo` (reported). Overlap: one open position per (cell, pool), cooldown 300 s after the exit decision (kit rule).
- Day = UTC date of the decision hour. First and last dates are partial; every date with >= 1 trade counts.

## IDEA 1: CALL-SYNC, cs_k12 + price-lag selector
Cell `cs12_lag` is the tested cell. Controls, priced only to read what the selector does and never promotable: `cs12_all` (no selector), `cs12_in` (complement of the selector).
- Burst (as DECLARE.md, K = 12): per pool, aligned 2-slot bucket `b = slot // 2`; consider BUY rows in the universe. Decision row = the first buy row of the bucket at which, counting rows of the bucket up to and including it, distinct buying traders >= 12 AND gross buy SOL >= 2.0. Decision slot = that row's slot. One trigger per (pool, bucket). Pool age >= 7,200 s since its first print in the block. (A bucket straddling an hour boundary is ignored: at most one bucket per hour.)
- Selector, decision-time fields only: `m = spot_post(decision row) / spot_pre(first buy row of the bucket) - 1`, with `spot_pre = (q + V) / b` PRE-trade of that first row and `spot_post = (q_post + V) / b_post`, `q_post = q + sol*(1 - 0.0125)`, `b_post = b - tok` of the decision row. `cs12_lag` keeps triggers with **m <= 0.02** (the burst has moved the price by at most 2% by the decision row: "not yet in price"); `cs12_in` keeps m > 0.02.
- Entry, exit, costs: as above, exits tp 1.12 / sl 0.92 / 900 s.
- Kill rules, applied mechanically to `cs12_lag` (deciding leg 160k / 1.9 s / 0.25 SOL / rent 0 / strand `total`):
  1. mean SOL per trade <= 0 under flat 15% OR under pressure slope 1 -> KILL.
  2. total ex-top-3 <= 0 (flat or pressure) -> KILL.
  3. total ex-best-date <= 0 (flat or pressure) -> KILL.
  4. fewer than half of the UTC dates with a positive total (pos_days * 2 < days; flat or pressure) -> KILL.
  5. median entry slip > 2% -> KILL, with slip = median over filled trips of `(Qe/Be) / decision spot - 1` at 1.9 s (Qe/Be is the fill print's pre-trade effective price).
  6. fewer than 10 admitted trades per day -> KILL (the CANDIDATES.md kill rule; also reported as `thin`).
- Also reported (not kills): n, mean SOL per trade under both models, both CI90 lower bounds, by-day totals, exit mix, guard-refused share, the selector's share of triggers, the same at 505k send and rent-charged. A "survivor" label is not an edge claim; it only means "worth one forward-paper declaration".
- Adjacency to breadth/retail acceleration (hunt23-b-flow-A, second-wave-2/4), asked for by CANDIDATES.md: shown only through the `cs12_all` / `cs12_in` / `cs12_lag` comparison; the 60 s breadth lines are not re-run.

## IDEA 2: SIZE-SKEW mirror as a VETO on FLUSH f2
Base rule chosen: **hunt29 I1 FLUSH, cell `f2`** (docs/library/hunt29-p4/p4rules.py `P4Rule._flush`, imported, not copied; the other three ideas in that file are stubbed out and the cells f3/f5 dropped). Why it: it is the simplest PumpSwap-WSOL entry in the repo that already has an exploration pricing-kit run (hunt29 P4; docs/library/hunt29-p4/SCREENS.md records FAIL on all cells, f2 flat -2.75% on four blocks at the same costs); it is one pattern with nested variants; it has by far the most trades (about 2,000 a day), which gives the veto power; and both sides of the tape are present around its trigger (the mirror ratio needs sells). WAKE and DROUGHT are unsuited (no sells in the window by construction). The base is a LOSING entry, so a positive result here would be a veto improving a losing book, not an edge.
- Mirror flag (veto), computed at the FLUSH f2 decision: over the pool's rows with `block_time` in `[bt_dec - 1800, bt_dec]` and `slot <= decision slot` (rolling 30 min window ending at the decision row, carried across hours): `nb >= 10` buys, `ns >= 10` sells, and `(sum buy SOL / nb) / (sum sell SOL / ns) <= 1/3`. Any other case: flag = 0. (DECLARE.md's counts used six aligned 300 s bins; this rolling form can be evaluated at any decision row; disclosed.)
- Base book = FLUSH f2 trades admitted by the kit under its own overlap rule (the veto does not free a pool for another trade: a vetoed trade is dropped from the priced base set; it does not change which other trades were admitted). Flagged set F, kept set K = base \ F, veto share `s = |F| / |base|`.
- Metric: lift `L = mean(K) - mean(base)` in SOL per trade under flat 15% and under pressure slope 1 (same base intercept).
- Random veto at matched share: paired bootstrap, 1,000 draws, seed 1 (`np.random.default_rng(1)`): each draw resamples base trades with replacement (trade level); `L_veto` uses the true flags of the resample, `L_rand` uses a random permutation of the flags inside the same resample (same share); statistic `D = L_veto - L_rand`. KILL if the 5th percentile of D <= 0 under flat OR under pressure. Also reported: 1,000 random vetoes (seed 1) on the actual sample (distribution of mean(K_rand)) and the date-clustered variant of D.
- Further kills: veto share < 10% of base entries (no information in the ratio, CANDIDATES.md) -> KILL; kept book K: kill rules 1-4 above (mean <= 0 under flat or pressure, ex-top-3, ex-best-date, half the dates). If the lift passes but K is still killed by 1-4, the verdict is "VETO INFORMATIVE, BOOK KILLED": the veto may be re-declared on another entry, never advanced as a book.
- Multiplicity: IDEA 2 is 1 base cell, 1 flag, 1 threshold. IDEA 1 is 1 burst cell, 1 selector threshold, plus 2 controls. No parameter moves after a price. A bug fix to match this text is logged as AMEND with its reason and the job rerun.
