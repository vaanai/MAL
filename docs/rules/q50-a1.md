DEC-031 Am.4 item 1.6 (hard halt). (DEC/DEC-031-forward-paper-arena.md:271)

> - **Dead-man.** The dead-man is `python -m tools.a3_halt deadman` (function `deadman`; constant `DEADMAN_S` = 48 h).
