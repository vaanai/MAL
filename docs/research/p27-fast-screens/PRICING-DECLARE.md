## Frozen rule: SERIAL-CREATOR (only priced idea)
Universe: non-Mayhem pump_bonding mints created in the pool (is_mayhem_mode false). Creator history: a mint "graduated" at its `complete` event (migrations). Flag a create if its creator had >= 2 graduated mints (complete block_time) in [create - 48 h, create). The 48 h history is left-censored at the pool start (2026-08-14T12), so early days are under-populated; disclosed, no correction.
Decision: first bonding trade of a flagged mint with age (block_time - create block_time) >= 30 s and post-trade progress p = (quote_reserve/1e9 - 30)/85.005359 < 0.25. One per mint. Counted days: UTC 2026-08-15 .. 2026-08-27 (13 days, as in #909); 08-14 (12 h) and 08-28 (12 h) are history only, no decision counted.
Entry model: decision at the decision print's slot D. The order lands at the first bonding print of the mint with slot >= D + 5 (1.9 s / 0.4 s per slot = 4.75, rounded up; the clean backfill has block_time and slot but no t_recv). If the mint has no such print before it completes, no trade (counted as unfilled; reported). Buy 0.25 SOL: fee 1.25% of input, constant product on the entry print's POST-trade virtual reserves (quote_reserve = virtual SOL, base_reserve = virtual tokens; convention verified in DECLARE.md: bonding rows are post-trade, k = 3.219e25 holds on the rows). Impact comes from this state.
Exit: sell all tokens against the last bonding print with slot <= entrySlot + 1500 + 5 (10-minute cap measured from the landing slot, plus the same 1.9 s lag), or the last bonding print before the mint's `complete`, whichever is first; sell fee 1.25% of output. Curve state is exact between prints, so the last print state is the sell state. Completed-before-cap trades are exited at the final curve state (a PumpSwap exit is not modelled; count reported). No stop, no take-profit.
Extra cost: 0.0001 SOL per send (landed buy, landed sell, failed buy), an assumption (not measured here); no ATA rent.
Fail models (landing failure of the BUY only; a failed buy costs the send cost and has no position; the sell is assumed to land):
  - flat 15%: per-trade expected SOL = 0.85 * pnl_landed - 0.15 * send_cost.
  - pressure-fail slope 1: tools/latency_curve.py FailCurve (b_slot 0.8, b_sol 0.35, scale 1, intercept fit so the mean fail on this book's sends is 0.289 via fit_curve). Pressure at entry = same_slot_buys = buys on the mint in the landing slot at or before the landing print; nearby = buy lamports on the mint in slots [landingSlot - 5, landingSlot] at or before the landing print (5 slots ~ 2 s; replaces the t_recv window). Expected SOL = (1 - p) * pnl_landed - p * send_cost.
Statistics (both fail models, each reported separately): n, mean SOL/trade, total SOL, bootstrap 90% CI of the mean (1000 draws, seed 1, 5th percentile = lower bound, resampling trades with replacement, random.Random(1) as in tools/latency_curve.py), total ex-top-3 trades, per-UTC-day table (n, total SOL), positive-day count of the 13, total with the best day removed, trades/day x mean net SOL x 0.25 SOL capacity line.

## Placebo (progress-matched, non-serial)
For every serial trade, one non-serial non-Mayhem mint's decision (same decision rule: first bonding trade with age >= 30 s and p < 0.25, creator NOT flagged), matched on UTC day and 0.05-wide progress bin of the decision print, drawn with random.Random(1) from a deterministic 1-in-3 (crc32(mint) % 3 == 0) subsample of the non-serial decisions, with replacement only if a cell is exhausted (count reported). Same entry, exit, costs, fail models. Reported: placebo n, mean, CI90 lower bound, and the paired mean difference serial minus placebo (bootstrap CI90). The placebo is context; it is not a kill rule, but a serial result that does not beat its placebo cannot be described as a creator-selection effect.

## Kill rules (from #909, applied mechanically)
KILL if ANY of: (a) mean SOL/trade <= 0 under flat 15% OR under pressure-fail slope 1; (b) total SOL ex-top-3 trades <= 0 (either fail model); (c) fewer than 5 of the 13 counted days have positive total SOL (either fail model). Survival of all three is NOT a gate pass: the promotion gate still needs >= 100 out-of-sample trades, >= 5 UTC days with a majority positive, CI lower bound > 0 and ex-top-3 > 0, under both models, on rows not used to choose the rule.

## Disclosures
1. In-sample exploration. The rule was chosen from a counts-only screen, then priced once here on a pool that earlier rules (G1 r7, entry models) were fit on. Any gate read needs forward paper rows.

That is a builder-sized PR chain (several PRs and a replay equivalence proof), not an afternoon.
3. Single priced pass. No parameter was tuned after seeing output; if the result is a KILL, the rule is closed for this form. Winner's curse: one rule, one pass, but selected from a six-idea screen.
