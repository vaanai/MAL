## 1. Mechanism
1. **Who loses, and why.** The rule is FLOOR's dip-buy (l1-floor-v2 logic) moved to PumpSwap graduates whose quote mint is not WSOL (USDC, pump-class tokens, other tokens): after a SELL print in a 24-200 h old pool whose quote vault is drained to <= 0.5 SOL-equivalent, whose spot fell more than 20% against 6 h ago and which is still alive (previous print <= 6 h, 24 h volume >= 1 SOL-equivalent), buy; take profit at +50% on the post-buy mark, else sell at 24 h; no stop. Sellers who dump a drained, quiet pool near its low lose to a buyer who holds through the rebound that new buyers bring. Holders keep doing it because these pools are thin and attention-driven: a small seller moves the spot a lot, and the sellers are mostly the creator's or early holders' exits. Other-quote pools add a second asset: the position is bought through a conversion leg (SOL → quote → token, and back), so the quote asset's own moves and fees are part of the result.
2. **What would stop it.** (a) A PumpSwap program or fee change (DEC-035 §4 redeploy); (b) a fee schedule on these pools that differs from the table's `pfee` (QP-RULING-1118-other-quote-counts notes 28 of 30 sampled other-quote graduations are synthetic completions with InitBoost; boosted fees possible); (c) the cohort ending: the 11-13 sample had 19 of 30 other-quote graduations from one creator in a 7 min burst; the counts show 11-11..11-13 USDC-only and pump-class pools from 11-14; (d) a quote collapse or depeg (pump-class quotes are memecoins). How we see it: daily trigger count from the driver and engine counters (`book/oq-status.json`), `fee_audit` counts, `entry_refused_*` (no quote state, no pool fee), and the drop-top-creator / drop-top-quote-mint lines at each read.
3. **FLOOR twin or sibling?** A sibling, not a twin: same decision logic, disjoint universe. The OQ host admits only pools with a known, non-WSOL quote mint (`tools/arena_oq_engine.oq_in_universe`); FLOOR trades only WSOL pools with V in [17.5, 17.7] SOL. **Pool overlap with FLOOR: zero by construction.** Minute overlap (same market hours, correlated dumps) is likely and is not measured; creators may overlap and are not measured.

## 3. What makes this sim believable
1. **Fills and latency.** Deciding cell: entry priced at the first print at or after decision + **4.085 s** (`LAT_PRICING_DECIDING_S`; DEC-035 Am.9 Erratum 2 latency set 3.010 / 4.085 / 5.590 s). FLOOR's live landing p50 is about 3.1 s (design report §4; DEC-035 Am.9 Erratum 2), so 4.085 s is slower than FLOOR's live median; that margin is the haircut for the live-minus-sim bias. The conversion leg is read at the same landing print (its own earlier landing is not modelled, declared gap 3). Exit at the first print at or after the exit decision + 0.55 s (`EXIT_LAG_MS`). The FLOOR control book's reconciliation status at drafting is "SIM OFF: not decision-grade" (LAB_STATE Now: recon through 2026-11-15T15:00Z, h3 FAIL, s1-s4 INSUFFICIENT); the manager states the status at S in the start record. Live fills have recently been better than the sim; no credit is taken for that.
2. **Costs.** `send_lamports` **505,000** per send, 4 sends per trip (quote buy, token buy, token sell, quote sell) = 2,020,000 lamports per trip, 2.02% of a 0.10 stake. 505,000 is the WSOL deciding stack's send-plus-priority figure (`SEND_FEE_DECIDING`), above FLOOR's live 160,000; QP on PR #1456 (bd723e9d) requires send >= 505,000 for parity, met. Pool fee: each pool's own fee from the table (`pfee`), on the token legs and, at the quote's own WSOL pool fee, on the conversion legs. Rent: report-only (`rent_report_lamports`, 2,039,280 per ATA, two ATAs), as RENT_REPORT in the WSOL stack; the deciding stack treats it as refunded. Price impact at the book's stake from V-priced reserves (below), at 0.10 and 0.25.
3. **PumpSwap pricing.** Every price is from the print's PRE-trade reserves with V included in Q, in the pool's quote units, converted through the quote's WSOL pool state at that print (`arena_oq_engine` header). Nothing on this card was priced before the 2026-10-04 V correction.
4. **Per-token fee check.** Per pool (`pfee` from the table), not one flat rate. Source per row (`arena_rule_l1_oq.price_fee`): a row carrying `creator_fee` with valid lp / protocol / amount fields is priced from its own fields (`fee_src_row`); a row without `creator_fee` (every tip-tape row, per the dry check) is priced WHOLE by the PumpSwap tier model (`tier_fee`: `pumpswap_sol_fee_ppm` by market cap in SOL-equivalent = (Q incl. V) / B × quote_mid × 1e6, the same model the WSOL open-sim books use; `fee_src_tier`); neither available gives no fee and no price (`fee_src_none`), never a 0 fee. The tier uses the pool's latest row (its pre-trade state) for both entry and exit legs (the WSOL stack tiers the exit at the post-sell state, a slightly higher fee; the difference from one 0.10 SOL fill is small). Not checked against landed fills: there are no live fills in this universe. `buyback_fee` is not on the stored tape row and is unmodelled. `fee_audit` (`rows`, `fee_src_row`, `fee_src_tier`, `fee_src_none`, `creator_fee_missing`, `buyback_fee_missing`) is reported at every read (declared gaps 5, 6, 16).
5. **Fail legs.** Both, by `tools/arena_oq_score.fail_legs`, which calls `arena_stack.fail_legs` unchanged (QP d31815db): flat 15% `(1-f)P - f·send` and pressure slope scale 1 with the intercept refit on this set. **The flat leg is the gate leg** (QP 85ae4c33: a book positive under pressure but not under flat FAILS; never quote a pressure-only positive).
6. **Trial count.** On other-quote outcomes: 0 cells priced before S. The rule constants are FLOOR's l1-floor-v2 constants (picked on WSOL data 2026-11-03..11-11, itself after a variant search: winner's curse carries over). The OQ-specific constants (V scaled band, vault and volume in SOL-equivalent, quote staleness 10 min USDC / 2 min other, exit wait cap 1 h, stale-exit haircut 0.5) were fixed in code (PRs #1450, #1455, #1456) before any outcome. Card 2 was ranked 2nd of 9 siblings on in-sample counts. This book watches 8 rows (2 stakes × 4 cells). Shrink: every figure is "best of <n_books_watched> rows; treat as shrunk", and the shrunk mean of §4 below.
7. **Concentration.** Every read shows: total after dropping the top 3 trades, per-UTC-day totals (day of the entry decision), total after dropping the top creator (trades with no creator are one group "unknown", counted) and after dropping the top quote mint, under both legs.
8. **Basis.** All scorer figures are net of sends, pool fees and conversion legs (the engine's P), then the fail blend. `filled_no_fail` is shown beside and labelled as having no fail blend.

**Declared gaps (on every read and every figure from this book).**
1. Priority fee beyond `send_lamports` is not modelled; 505,000 per send carries priority as the WSOL deciding stack does.
2. A fee-tier change between the quote leg and the token leg is not modelled (second order; the landing-row fee is used).
3. The conversion leg's own earlier landing is not modelled: both legs read the same landing print.
4. **G1:** `_stale_exit` (an exit capped after 1 h without a usable quote state) and `_score_unfilled` (an entry that never landed in 24 h) apply no gap test in either gap mode and never set `gap_in_hold`. In the strict book a capped stale exit whose hold spans a gap is still scored, and the strict share misses it. The read reports `stale_exit_capped*` and `entry_unfilled_*` counts next to the strict share.
5. `buyback_fee` is not on the tape row: unmodelled; `fee_audit.buyback_fee_missing` is reported.
6. **Creator fee (rewritten in v2).** A row without `creator_fee` is priced from the tier model (`fee_src_tier`), never with the creator leg at 0; a row with neither its own fee fields nor a tier fee (no usable reserves or no quote price) prices nothing (`fee_src_none`) and the entry or exit is refused or waits as before. `fee_audit.fee_src_row` / `fee_src_tier` / `fee_src_none` and `creator_fee_missing` are reported at every read.
7. **Static vs live quote prices.** The counts screen (job #3048) converted quote units with a static quote price file (sha 2678b75c); this book converts with the quote's own WSOL pool state at the print (post-trade approximation 1.25%, `quote_post_fallback` counted). Trigger counts in the book can therefore differ from the screen's.
8. **Undercount from unpriced quote mints.** A pool whose quote has no resolvable WSOL pool state is dropped at the rule's `quote_price_known` stage (no decision, so not in the excluded share), and a landing without a quote state is refused (`refused` row, counted in the board share). The book sees only quote mints with a live WSOL pool.
9. The stranded-quote leg (token buy fails after the quote buy lands) is not inside the read; the scorer's `sensitivity_stranded_quote_leg` gives it at 0 (= the read) and at full loss (upper bound), both legs (QP D3).
10. The unfilled round trip prices both legs at the decision-time quote state; quote drift over 24 h is not modelled. The pressure inputs of the conversion legs are the token pool's.
11. Latency twins share ENTRY decisions only: a take-profit decided before a twin's entry lands is refused (SELL_EARLY) and retried, so twin P&L differences include exit-timing changes, not pricing alone; no decision-md5 equality across twins is claimed.
12. **Code pin coverage.** `osb_follow`'s `code.sha256` covers osb_follow.py, its helpers and floor_lane_host pins (`verify_pins()`: arena_rules_v2, arena_declog, arena_state, arena_pool_table, arena_rules), NOT the OQ files (`osb_follow_oq.py`, `arena_rule_l1_oq.py`, `arena_oq_engine.py`). Compensation: every start first checks `git rev-parse HEAD` = 74c5ba9c4ca82d633387478e7f7e366a8792f42f, an empty `git status --porcelain`, and the sha256 of those three files and `tools/paper_curve_math.py` (a dependency of the rule since f38137ef, also outside `code.sha256`) against §10's list (refuse on any difference); the per-hour `ledger_md5` restart check still catches any pricing change in replayed hours. The scorer is checked at each read against the reader pin (§2).
13. The counts ruling's input had 13 broken and 5 missing hours around 2026-11-08 and before (QP-RULING-1118-other-quote-counts). The keeper status read at 2026-11-18T20:24:45Z (`keep/.keeper-status.json`) shows keep contiguous from 2026-11-03T03 to 2026-11-18T19 (377 h, no missing or failed hours); any feed gaps inside those hours lie in the seed, at least 10 days before S, and touch the 6 h / 24 h state windows only for pools whose prints fall next to them.
14. No live control book exists for other-quote pools: under DEC-035 Am.9 §3 every figure is "NOT COMPARABLE: not decision-grade" for any live step.
15. **Age and V reference are left-censored at `seed_from_hour`.** `OQTable` sets a pool's `first` and `vref` at the first print the table sees (`tools/arena_rule_l1_oq.py`, about l.200), and the age test is `bt - tab.first[pool]` (about l.357). A pool whose real first print is before the seed start and whose first print inside the seed comes x hours after the seed start looks younger than it is, and its V reference is taken mid-life; it can pass the 24-200 h age test at S + t while t <= 200 h − L + x, with L the seed length. Decision (fixed now): the seed starts at **2026-11-03T03**, the keeper's earliest contiguous hour (the WSOL osb books' seed), not at S − 201 h, so L is about 380 h for S near 2026-11-19 and only pools silent for at least L − 200 h (about 180 h, 7.5 days) before their first seed print can be mis-aged; with S − 201 h that exposure would have covered nearly the whole L1 window. **Bias:** such pools are older, revived pools outside the rule's 24-200 h cohort, with a mid-life V reference that can move the scaled-V band test either way; they add positions the rule would not take, with an unknown P&L sign (quiet, drained pools that revive are this book's case). **The L1 read is exposed**, reduced to pools silent for 7.5 days or more inside the seed; their number is not measured (no counter exists). `first` is never swept, so the effect fades as S recedes. Declared on every read (disclosure 3).
16. **Fee tier schedule on other-quote pools (new in v2; unverified).** The SOL-pool tier schedule (`pumpswap_sol_fee_ppm`, total 0.30% at the top tier to 1.25% at the bottom) is applied to other-quote pools by SOL-equivalent market cap, and also to the quote's own WSOL conversion pool (quote_mid 1.0; for a non-pump base that "market cap" has no meaning and lands at the top tier, 0.30%). Direction: probably conservative, not proven. Off-schedule PumpSwap pools have historically charged LP 0.20% + protocol 0.05% with no creator leg, at or below the 0.30% floor; if pump tiers other-quote pools by market cap in QUOTE units, a USD-stable quote gives a higher tier and a lower fee than modelled. It is optimistic if a quote is worth more than 1 SOL per unit or if pump applies a separate higher schedule to other-quote pools. For the conversion pool the tier replaces the row's own lp + protocol fields, which were exact for a pool with no creator leg. No counter shows how often the tier fee is below the row's own parts (QP's suggested `fee_tier_below_row_parts` is not in the code at 74c5ba9c). **Must be checked against pump's on-chain FeeConfig before any gate read, belief record or live use** (§4 pre-read condition).

## 4. Believe-the-sim criteria for this book
The deciding read is §11's look (L1, or L2 when L1 has too few trades). All of these must hold on the **strict book `osb-oq-v1` at 0.10 SOL**, entries with decision in [S, L):

- **Shrunk net sim mean per trade** under the flat leg and the pressure leg, and **μ0 at 0.10** (pressure leg, winsorized at the sim's own 2.5th / 97.5th percentiles of x, net at 0.10, at the deciding cell): all three > 0 (DEC-036 §4 item 2). Shrinkage, declared now: m = (n · mean(x) + n0 · μp) / (n + n0), x = SOL per trade / stake, n0 = 25, μp = −c̄ with c̄ = max(c, measured mean round-trip cost per trade / stake), where c is arithmetic from DEC-036 §8 estimates, not a measurement (the measured value, read prerequisite P2 in §11, is used when larger):
  - at 0.10: sends 4 × 505,000 = 2,020,000 lamports = 2.02% of 0.10 SOL; token round trip pool fee 2.4% (DEC-036 §8 estimate, 1.2% per leg); conversion round trip pool fee 2.4% (the same estimate applied to the quote's WSOL pool); token-leg impact 1.105% (DEC-036 §8 model, 2 s² / (18 + s) at s = 0.10). **c = 2.02 + 2.4 + 2.4 + 1.105 = 7.925%, μp = −max(0.07925, measured).**
  - at 0.25 (wherever a shrunk 0.25 figure is printed): sends 2,020,000 lamports = 0.808% of 0.25 SOL; 2.4 + 2.4 pool fees; token-leg impact 2.740%. **c = 0.808 + 2.4 + 2.4 + 2.740 = 8.348%, μp = −max(0.08348, measured).**
  - Conversion-leg price impact is excluded from c and declared; rent is excluded (report-only, refunded in the deciding stack).
- **Deciding latency cell:** 4.085 s (the strict book's default; the twins are reported beside it, never instead of it).
- **Criteria:**
  1. Minimum evidence: **>= 7 complete UTC days AND >= 40 closed trades** at 0.10 in the window. At <= 8 trigger pools/day (an upper bound from the screen) with 24 h holds and one position per pool, 100 trades take at least 2 weeks; 40 is the floor below which no belief is written, not a gate count.
  2. Flat leg: total > 0 and total ex-top-3 > 0. Pressure leg: total > 0 and total ex-top-3 > 0.
  3. Flat leg: total after dropping the top creator > 0 and total after dropping the top quote mint > 0 (the 11-13 one-creator burst; class concentration in the counts). Pressure-leg values reported.
  4. Flat leg: a majority of the UTC days with trades are positive.
  5. Exclusions: the board-style share ((excluded + refused) / requested) <= 5% AND the strict any-gap-in-hold share <= 5%; either above 5% → **NOT_DECIDABLE** (not BELIEVE, not RETIRE). G1 counts are reported next to the strict share; if the positions that are `stale_exit_capped` or `entry_unfilled` (a position in both counted once) exceed 5% of the requested entries (the same denominator as the board share), the read is also NOT_DECIDABLE (they bypass the gap test).
  6. **Pre-read condition (fee schedule, gap 16):** before any gate read, belief record or live use of this universe, the SOL-pool tier schedule as applied here is checked against pump's on-chain PumpSwap FeeConfig (read-only `getAccountInfo` of the fee program's config: tiers keyed on market cap, flat fees for non-canonical pools, and how the cap is denominated) and pump's published fee docs, and the result is recorded; until then a BELIEVE verdict licenses nothing beyond that check.
  7. Reported, not criteria: the stranded-quote full-loss sensitivity (the belief record must say whether the flat total survives it), the gap-in-hold twin, the 3.010 / 5.590 twins, the 0.25 cell, `fee_audit` with its fee sources, `pending`, and the CLAUDE.md gate items under both legs (n >= 100, >= 5 days with a majority positive, CI lower bound of mean SOL/trade > 0 at 1,000 draws seed 1 5th percentile, ex-top-3 > 0), labelled "gate items reported; an open simulation is not a gate read (DEC-035 Am.9 §1)".
